01 — About

About

Where I started, and why I went back to study.

I spent a decade building systems that had to keep running — finance and logistics workflows on mainframes at Nissan, then data pipelines and predictive models, then machine learning for demand forecasting and financial crime detection at KBC Bank. That work ran from Chennai, Paris and Amsterdam.

I came back to full-time study to go deeper on the modelling side, and completed an MSc in Computer Science at Leiden University. My thesis is on visual entailment: decomposing a claim about an image into atomic facts so a frozen vision-language model can be checked one fact at a time, rather than trusted for a single verdict.

What carries across from the first decade is a habit of reporting what actually happened. Every repository on my GitHub leads with the result — including the experiments that contradicted the method — and ships the full report alongside the code.

Reading Analog Clocks with CNNs

Five ways of representing clock time for the same convolutional network, on the same 18,000 images.

Changing only the output representation moved error from 182.93 minutes — chance — to 8.52. The most precise-looking formulation was the worst.
